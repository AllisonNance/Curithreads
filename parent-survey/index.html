The Product

Curithreads shirts explain different "how does that work?" ideas from science and engineering. Each one comes with its own scan experience.

On the Shirt

A diagram they can trace with a finger.

  1. 01

    The Sun

    Light from the sun shines down on the solar panels.

  2. 02

    Solar Panels

    The panels turn the sunlight into electricity.

  3. 03

    Inverter

    Electricity is changed from DC electricity to AC electricity.

  4. 04

    EV Charger & Battery

    The charger fills the car’s battery so it can go.

Scanning the QR

The shirt opens a page that keeps the explanation going.

Point a phone camera at the QR code and a web page opens. No download, no account, no distractions. Here’s what's on it:

  • Watch

    A series of 90 second to 2 minute low-stimulation videos break down the scientific concepts in the shirt diagram.

  • Play

    Interact by completing simple activities or games.

  • Try

    Try an experiment or observe in real life.
